# N.M.R. — Neuro-Microbial Response

## Status
CANON foundation; deeper faction-system details still in development.

## Identity
NMR is an international / independent specialist biological-response force, not the military arm of a specific country.

## Season 2 Story Role
- Arrives in Chernarus after local containment fails.
- Initially performs genuine rescue, containment and support operations.
- Establishes its Base of Operations at Rify.
- Discovers pathogen-control potential and survivor resistance.
- Gradually prioritizes research and control over civilian protection.

## Core Progression
**Rescue → Containment → Research → Control**

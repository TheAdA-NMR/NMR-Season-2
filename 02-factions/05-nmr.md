# N.M.R. — Neuro-Microbial Response

## Status
CANON foundation; deeper faction-system details still in development.

## Identity
NMR is an international / independent specialist biological-response force, not the military arm of a specific country.

## Season 2 Story Role
- Arrives in Chernarus after local containment fails.
- Initially performs genuine rescue, containment and support operations.
- Establishes its Base of Operations at **Rify**.
- Chooses Rify partly because the nearby **Rify Shipwreck contaminated zone** provides direct access to an active contamination environment.
- Uses the contaminated zone for continuous biological sampling, observation and experimentation.
- Research conducted around the shipwreck directly supports NMR's continuing **biological-weapons and biological-enhancement research**.
- Uses the Rify bunker to secure and study samples, research data and recovered materials taken from contaminated areas.
- Discovers pathogen-control potential and survivor resistance.
- Gains access to the true purpose, advanced research and biological enhancement potential of the Pavlovo program.
- Recognizes the strategic value of the recovered technology, biological knowledge and enhancement capability.
- Decides that these assets must remain under **exclusive NMR control**.
- Begins recovering and securing related research, equipment, samples and technical knowledge across Chernarus.
- Gradually treats outside access to this technology as a threat.
- Prioritizes research, control and monopoly over civilian protection.

## Core Progression
**Rescue → Containment → Research → Control → Monopoly**

## Core Motivation
NMR initially enters Chernarus to stop the outbreak. After discovering what the Pavlovo program was capable of, its objective changes.

NMR no longer wants only to contain the crisis. It wants to control the knowledge and power created by it.

The technology, biological research and enhancement potential discovered in Chernarus are considered too valuable and too dangerous to be shared. NMR therefore seeks to place all related assets under its own authority and prevent rival groups from obtaining them.

Rify becomes central to this objective because it gives NMR a secure bunker, maritime logistics and immediate access to a contaminated environment where biological-weapons and enhancement research can continue under its exclusive control.

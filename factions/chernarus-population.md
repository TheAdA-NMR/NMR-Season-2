# Chernarus Population

## Status
CANON concept; detailed mechanics pending.

This is the fifth major social group in Season 2.

It represents people who do not belong to any organized faction:
- independent survivors
- civilians
- traders
- locals
- unaffiliated workers and small communities

This group is not intended to function like the three player-controlled factions. It represents the remaining independent population of Chernarus and can serve as the recruitment pool, civilian population and neutral social layer of the world.

# Season 2 Chapters

## Chapter 1 — Before the Outbreak
Normal Chernarus life, classified military/scientific activity and subtle warning signs.

## Chapter 2 — The Outbreak
Pavlovo containment breach, first infections, Balota recovery operation and wider spread.

## Chapter 3 — NMR Deployment
NMR arrives as a genuine rescue/containment force and establishes operations from Rify.

## Chapter 4 — The Change
NMR discovers pathogen-control potential and survivor resistance. Priorities begin to shift.

## Chapter 5 — The Secret
Players uncover what NMR is researching, why civilians became secondary and what the organization is ultimately protecting.

## Chapter 6 — Operation: No Man's Road
Containment/isolation doctrine and the meaning of the server identity become part of the lore.

## Finale
The Chernarus operation reaches its endgame reveal. Season 2 is completed entirely inside Chernarus.

## Season 3 Hook
A future Map Link / transfer device can connect Season 2 endgame progression to another map, but the destination and Season 3 design remain intentionally unresolved.

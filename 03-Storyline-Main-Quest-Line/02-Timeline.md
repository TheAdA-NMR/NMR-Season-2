# Story Timeline

1. **Before the Outbreak** — Chernarus appears broadly normal while classified military biological research is conducted at Pavlovo.
2. **Pavlovo Containment Breach** — the biological experiment loses containment and personnel are infected.
3. **Balota Recovery Operation** — teams from Balota recover biological material and advanced weapons-development equipment from Pavlovo and transfer them to the Balota underground bunker.
4. **Secondary Spread** — recovery personnel, nearby people and wildlife are exposed during the transfer, accelerating the spread beyond Pavlovo.
5. **Containment Collapse** — local military, medical and emergency resources can no longer contain the outbreak.
6. **NMR Deployment** — Chernarus requests specialist international assistance; Neuro-Microbial Response arrives with a genuine humanitarian and containment mission.
7. **NMR Humanitarian Operations** — NMR conducts rescue, quarantine, evacuation, medical support, decontamination and containment operations across Chernarus.
8. **Rify Base of Operations** — NMR establishes its main Chernarus headquarters around the Rify bunker, contaminated shipwreck zone and dedicated port.
9. **Research Expansion & Discovery** — findings from research facilities and the Rify contaminated zone reveal the military, technological and biological potential of the Pavlovo program.
10. **NMR Turning Point** — the discoveries change the mindset of NMR leadership. NMR begins looking beyond its original mission and starts considering how this technology and biological power could be controlled and used to shape the post-collapse world.
11. **Control Operations** — NMR begins seeking, recovering and securing research, technology, biological materials and related knowledge across Chernarus, while increasingly preventing outside groups from gaining access to them.
12. **Season 2 Endgame — PENDING TEAM DISCUSSION** — the exact ending is not yet finalized. Season 2 may conclude with the final Chernarus operation, or, if the Map Link system is ready and approved by the team, the storyline may continue beyond Chernarus through access to a second map. The exact transition, access requirements and endgame progression will be decided later.

# Season 2 Chapters

## Player Entry Point & Narrative Structure

Players enter the Season 2 world **after the events of Chapter 3 have already taken place**.

The chapter structure therefore works on two parallel narrative tracks:

- **Chapters 1–3 — Past / Discovery:** These events are historical background. Players gradually reconstruct what happened before their arrival by finding lore documents, records, environmental evidence and other in-world information.
- **Chapters 4–5 — Present / Active Questline:** These events form the main playable Season 2 quest progression. Players take part in the current conflict while discovering what NMR is doing across Chernarus.
- **Chapter 6 — Endgame Direction:** The final operational phase of the Season 2 storyline. Exact quest structure, final facility and final confrontation will be developed under Quest Progression.

This means players are not told the complete history at the beginning. While progressing through the active questline, they simultaneously uncover the truth about Chapters 1–3.

## Chapter 1 — Before the Outbreak
Normal Chernarus life, classified military/scientific activity at Pavlovo and subtle warning signs before the crisis.

**Player discovery:** Historical lore only. Players learn about this period through documents and evidence found during later quests and exploration.

## Chapter 2 — The Outbreak
The Pavlovo containment breach, first infections, Balota recovery operation, secondary spread and the collapse of local containment.

**Player discovery:** Historical lore only. Players gradually reconstruct how Zvirus escaped containment and how the Balota recovery operation helped spread the outbreak.

## Chapter 3 — NMR Deployment
NMR arrives in Chernarus as a genuine rescue and containment force, conducts humanitarian operations and establishes the Rify Base of Operations.

**Player starting point:** By the time the player enters the Season 2 world, these events have already happened. The player discovers the details of NMR's original mission and early actions retrospectively through lore.

## Chapter 4 — Research & The Turning Point
Research from Rify and other locations reveals the military, technological and biological potential of the Pavlovo program. NMR leadership begins moving beyond its original humanitarian mission.

**Active questline:** Players begin investigating current NMR operations while uncovering evidence of how and why NMR's priorities changed.

## Chapter 5 — Control Operations
NMR expands its operations across Chernarus, seeking and securing research, technology, biological materials and related knowledge while preventing outside groups from gaining access.

**Active questline:** Players investigate, infiltrate, disrupt or confront NMR operations and gradually understand the organization's current objectives.

## Chapter 6 — Operation: No Man's Road
The Season 2 storyline moves into its final operational phase.

The exact meaning of **Operation: No Man's Road**, the final facility, final confrontation, rewards and any possible Map Link transition will be defined under `04-Quest-Progression.md`.

## Finale — PENDING TEAM DISCUSSION

The exact Season 2 ending is not yet finalized. The storyline may conclude with the final Chernarus operation, or, if the Map Link system is ready and approved by the team, it may continue beyond Chernarus.

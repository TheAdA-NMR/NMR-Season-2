# Economy & Traders

## Discussion / Design Notes

> These are **working economy design targets** for Season 2 and remain open for team discussion. They are not final implementation rules yet.

## Ground Loot & Weapon Progression

- Keep **SNAFU weapons** as limited as practical in normal ground loot.
- SNAFU weapons should primarily be obtained through **events, AI missions and other progression-based activities** rather than routine map looting.
- The intention is to preserve weapon progression and make higher-value weapons feel earned rather than immediately available.

## Gear & Carrying-Capacity Progression

- Add a clearer progression step between **mid-tier clothing/gear** and end-game gear.
- High-capacity storage gear should not be obtainable too quickly through normal progression.
- A player should not realistically reach something equivalent to a **1000-capacity backpack after only one week of play**.
- Carrying capacity should increase gradually as part of long-term character and gear progression.

## Trader Economy & Money Progression

- Slow the **trader-driven economy** as much as practical so progression is not mainly based on quickly buying everything from traders.
- Make **working, jobs and active gameplay-based income** more attractive and meaningful.
- Traders should support the economy without becoming the easiest or fastest path to every important item.

## Player-to-Player Economy

- Encourage **P2P trading** more strongly as part of the Season 2 economy.
- The economy should create reasons for players to exchange materials, crafted items, rare loot, services and progression resources with each other instead of relying only on NPC traders.

## Vehicle Acquisition

- As a general direction, **vehicles should not simply spawn as freely available assets**; they should be obtained through normal gameplay progression, economy, quests, events or other designed acquisition paths.
- One possible exception for discussion is a very small amount of **starter coastal mobility**, for example only **1 boat and 2 quad bikes across the entire coast**.
- Whether any such free coastal vehicle spawns are used at all remains open for discussion.

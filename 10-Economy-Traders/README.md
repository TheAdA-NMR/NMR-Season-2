# Economy & Traders

## Discussion / Design Notes

> Working S02 economy targets — open for team discussion, not final rules.

## Loot & Gear Progression

- Keep **SNAFU weapons** minimal in ground loot; primary sources should be **events and AI missions**.
- Add a clear **mid-tier → end-game gear** progression step.
- High-capacity gear should take time to earn; a **1000-capacity backpack** should not be reachable after roughly one week of play.

## Economy & Trading

- Slow the **trader economy** and make **jobs / active gameplay income** more valuable.
- Encourage **P2P trading** and reduce dependence on NPC traders.
- Increase **Black Market difficulty** and make access/travel to it more challenging.

## Vehicle Progression

- Vehicles should be earned through gameplay rather than freely spawned.
- Possible exception for discussion: only **1 coastal boat + 2 quad bikes** as limited starter mobility.

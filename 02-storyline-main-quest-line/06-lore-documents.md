# Lore Documents

Lore Documents belongs under **Story / Quest Line**.

This document will define how the Season 2 story is delivered in-game through fragmented evidence rather than a single exposition source.

## Player Discovery Structure

Players enter the Season 2 world after the events covered by **Chapters 1–3** have already taken place.

Chapters 1–3 therefore function as the historical background of the world rather than as a directly playable quest sequence. While progressing through the active **Chapter 4–5** questline, players gradually reconstruct this earlier history through lore documents and other in-world evidence discovered across Chernarus.

The lore should reveal the past in fragments:

- **Chapter 1 — Before the Outbreak** — normal life in Chernarus, classified activity at Pavlovo and early warning signs.
- **Chapter 2 — The Outbreak** — the Pavlovo containment breach, the Balota recovery operation, secondary spread and collapse of containment.
- **Chapter 3 — NMR Deployment** — NMR's arrival in Chernarus, its genuine humanitarian / containment operations and the establishment of the Rify Base of Operations.

Players should not receive the complete history from one source. Different documents, records, locations, environmental clues and NPC information should reveal different parts of Chapters 1–3, allowing the player to understand the past while simultaneously participating in the current Chapter 4–5 events.

## Planned Categories

- **Civilian** — newspapers, diaries, witness accounts
- **Medical** — hospital records, patient reports, pathology notes
- **Military** — situation reports, recovery manifests, logistics records
- **NMR** — NMR mission orders, field reports, command directives
- **Scientific** — biological analysis, pathogen research, experiment notes

## Design Rule

No single document should reveal the entire truth. Important conclusions should emerge from multiple sources, locations and chapters.

The detailed narrative quest structure and the way story information is shared with players inside the game world will be developed here. This includes documents, records, environmental clues, NPC information and other in-world storytelling methods.

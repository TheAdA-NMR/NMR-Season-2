# Season 2 Factions

Factions is a **server-wide Season 2 system** and is intentionally kept separate from the Story / Quest Line.

The Story / Quest Line references faction information from this section only when needed for narrative or quest design.

## Five Main Groups

1. **Player Faction 1 — Security / Order**
2. **Player Faction 2 — Medical / Cure**
3. **Player Faction 3 — Hunters / Farmers / Self-Sufficiency**
4. **N.M.R. — Neuro-Microbial Response**
5. **Chernarus Population — unaffiliated civilians / survivors / traders / locals**

## Current Design Direction

### Security / Order
Purpose: protection, patrol, settlement security, trade-route protection and order.

### Medical / Cure
Purpose: medical care, outbreak research, treatment and cure development.

This faction should ideologically contrast with NMR:

**Medical Faction: Cure it.**

**NMR: Control it.**

### Hunters / Farmers
Purpose: hunting, farming, fishing, food production, self-sufficiency and survival through the land.

### NMR
Story faction with its own Chernarus operation and research agenda.

### Chernarus Population
People who remain independent from organized factions.
